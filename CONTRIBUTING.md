# Contributing

This repository is **EchoMemory's public home**: documentation, releases,
issues, and ideas. The source code is developed privately and is not
published here. That's a deliberate decision, not an oversight.

What we warmly welcome here:

- **Bug reports**: the issue form asks for the Output log (View > Output >
  EchoMemory); it narrates every note saved, every approval and every answer,
  and usually names the cause.
- **Ideas**: what should Milo remember, or do, that he doesn't yet? When you
  ask Milo for something he can't do, he offers to draft an email to
  milo@echotools.dev; that works too.
- **Doc fixes**: typos and unclear sentences in these files. PRs welcome.

Reports for the whole EchoTools suite (EchoVoice, EchoAvatar and EchoMemory)
are also read at
[github.com/PiGON-AI/echotools](https://github.com/PiGON-AI/echotools/issues).

Questions about setup? Ask Milo: he answers from a guide that ships with
the extension. Or read `AI-PARTNER.md` with your AI coding partner.
