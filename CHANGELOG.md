# Changelog

Every change, in plain language.

## 1.1.0, Thanksgiving

> **Milo says:** Hello Code Peeps! Three new voices joined us: the Pilgrim, the Pumpkin and a Turkey, and I dress up as each of them. You can preview any voice before you pick it, and OpenAI and Fish Audio voices can read to you with your own key. I keep your days now too: reminders, a calendar, meetings and countdowns to the holidays, and you can choose Claude as my brain. And a brand new extension: meet EchoLive, video meetings right inside your editor, with screen sharing, chat and files, and I can join you in the room. Happy Thanksgiving, Code Peeps!

- **Reminders that ring.** Pin a reminder from the EchoMemory menu, or just ask Milo ("remind me in 2 minutes to check the build"): asking is the yes, so he sets it at once, says the moment back, and offers Undo. He rings it at the time you chose, while VS Code is open: his window shows it and he says it. Mark it Done or snooze it for 10 minutes, 1 hour, or until tomorrow at 9 am.
- **Milo knows your days.** Ask "what meetings did I have today?" or "what's on tomorrow?" and he answers from his calendar: reminders, meetings, calls and holidays. Ask what happened in a call and he finds its meeting notes. To answer, the reminders and meetings he reads go to the brain you chose, like your notes do; with Ollama on your machine they stay with you.
- **Milo's calendar.** Milo's Memory has two tabs now, Inbox and Calendar. The Calendar shows what is due now, each day ahead by time, your meetings with a Join button, the holidays, and what you finished this week. The Calendar button in Milo's menu opens it straight away.
- **Reminders in the Inbox.** A Reminders section sits above the notes waiting for you: overdue first, then coming up, meetings marked.
- **Countdowns.** Ask Milo "how long till Christmas?" and he answers for every US federal holiday and Halloween. Once a day he brings up the next holiday by himself when it is a week away or less, and your meetings in the next three days. Pure date math on your machine.
- **Meetings from EchoLive.** A meeting you book in EchoLive becomes a reminder at its exact moment, in your own time zone, with Join. Weekly meetings show once, marked "every" and the day. In a call, only meeting reminders speak; the others wait quietly on screen.
- **Meeting notes go to your Inbox.** When an EchoLive call ends, its notes arrive as one note waiting for your approval, and so does anything you ask Milo to remember during the call. Nothing is kept until you approve it.
- **Contacts, kept on this machine.** The people you invite in EchoLive are remembered so you type each address once. Never in your project or your notes, never synced; **Forget contacts (kept on this machine)** clears them.
- **Claude as Milo's brain.** **Choose Milo's brain model** now lists Claude beside Copilot and Ollama, with your own Anthropic API key: Milo asks for the key once, checks it with Anthropic, and keeps it in VS Code's secret store. What you say to Milo, and the notes he reads to answer, go to Anthropic under your key and are billed to your Anthropic account. Ask Milo still uses no AI.
- **A new icon.**
- **Milo's help guide knows the Thanksgiving release**: EchoVoice 1.3.0, EchoAvatar 1.3.0 and EchoLive 0.17.14 (its first release, a preview), with the releases before them.
